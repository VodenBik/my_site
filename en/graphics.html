Approach

How I work with graphics

01

Purpose and medium first

A layout for print, website, presentation or social networks requires different logic. I define the context first, then choose the composition and format.

02

Clean build

The final file should not only be beautiful, but also technically neat: grid, padding, sizes, fonts, export and readability.

03

One style on all materials

Even individual banners, slides and PDFs should look like part of one system, and not like random pictures from different folders.
