Structure and visual language

Presentations

Systematic presentation of complex information: script, hierarchy, diagrams and a single visual language from the title slide to the final conclusion.

Selection

15 projects

Format

Artboards 16:9

Tasks

Sales and training

Skills

Scenario, grid, infographic

Here are selected slides from various presentations. They show how I build structure, visual hierarchy and a unified style for the tasks of business, sales, training and communication.

Autonomous marketing department

The material is constructed as a commercial proposal for the creation of a marketing department. Its purpose is to explain the scope of work, stages and pricing. A dark grid, orange markers, charts, and cards organize dense information.

Corporate rafting

The presentation describes the corporate rafting program and the Buno Camp site. The task is to show the concept, route of the day, conditions and cost structure. Photos, timeline and icons are combined into a natural green-blue system.

HR 24/5

The presentation introduces the areas of work of the HR expert and examples of completed tasks. It helps to consistently show services, cases and reasons for trust. Light grid, purple accents and short semantic blocks support the business tone.

Premium pools

The material presents the design, construction and maintenance of private swimming pools. The purpose of the presentation is to address common concerns, compare technologies and explain the stages of work. Dark architectural style, tables and a linear process diagram form a seamless premium presentation.

Real estate affiliate program

The English-language material is structured as an invitation to partnership in the Turkish real estate sector. Slides explain experience, portfolio, example property, workflow and commission model. Purple gradients, bold statements, and a step-by-step outline make the proposal quick to read.

True Shift

A corporate presentation reveals the services of a recruitment agency. Its purpose is to identify the personnel problem and explain the benefits of external recruiting. The blue visual system, statistical blocks and thematic images support a coherent argument.

Wood Brook range

The material systematizes the collection of bags and accessories by purpose and audience. Its task is to show the breadth of the range, basic colors and current shapes. A warm neutral palette, product cards and large collages create a calm catalog presentation.

Share sale

The investment proposal is devoted to the sale of a stake in a group of companies associated with the production of beverages. The task is to present the company, the business structure and the main terms of the offer. The black and red palette, large portrait visuals and linear infographics set a strong commercial character.

TTV

The presentation describes personnel selection and outsourcing services. It leads from the employer's problems to the solution, the advantages of the format and getting to know the team. Circular compositions, pictograms and a single blue and white palette connect different types of information.

Liana

The material presents Liana as an automated information system for managing work processes. Slides identify the audience, formulate needs and reveal the benefits of the product. The dark blue system, large 3D figure and simple diagrams help explain the service without being overwhelming.

Real estate sales in Turkey

The material was prepared as a speech on the sale of real estate in Turkey through arguments for realtors. Its goal is to clear up doubts, compare markets and show the benefits of the partnership model. The white background, blue-orange accents and dialogue blocks make the logic of the speech clear.

International partnership

The presentation explains the model of cooperation between a Russian realtor and a local partner in Turkey. The task is to show the client’s request, the roles of the parties and the value of joint support of the transaction. Editorial grids, architectural photographs, and compact comparison charts support an expert tone.

Real Estate Strategy 2026

The strategic briefing examines Turkish real estate as a tool for protecting capital in a volatile environment. The slide system helps to compare purchase motives, object criteria and legalization options. A dark palette, circular diagrams and sequential frames collect analytical material into a single scenario.

Konak Construction

The catalog presentation introduces the developer and a selection of residential projects. Its goal is to show the geography, parameters of the complexes and features of the infrastructure. Monochrome photographs, red accents and modular cards form a strict premium system.

Fortress Investment

The corporate review presents the directions of Fortress Investment's work in the Turkish real estate market. The material explains geography, services, partner network and the overall customer journey. Information strips, photographs and a signature blue palette hold a large volume of facts in a single system.
