About the project

Working e-commerce without heavy development

Isralend is an online grocery store. The site is made for convenient selection of products, placing an order and placing an order without a complex CRM: the user goes from the catalog to the cart, and the order is automatically sent to Telegram and email.

The project was assembled as a practical e-commerce tool with an emphasis on launch speed, clear structure and further scaling of the assortment.

Interface

Catalog, cards and ordering

The main screen shows the product structure of the site: categories, product cards, cart and ordering script in one clear user flow.

Vibe coding

Fast iterations to a working site

The project was assembled in vibe coding format: from the idea and structure of the catalog to the functional logic of the shopping cart, multilingualism and sending orders. This approach made it possible to quickly get not merely a mockup, but a functional website, ready to be tested with real users.
