Task

Why a media kit?

Elena Boyarko is a bag designer, speaker in the fashion and style industry, founder of the Accessories Design Bureau. She regularly speaks at conferences and collaborates with brands and media.

The goal was a document that presents the speaker on its own: open it, read it, and understand the value immediately. No unnecessary calls or clarifications.

A media kit is not a resume. This is a pitch deck for a personal brand.

Key difference from the standard CV, which determined the approach to the structure

Brainstorm

Packaging approach

Organizers need clear answers, not a list of courses: who she is, what she is talking about, what the audience will receive at the speech and how to contact her.

01

Three roles - three audiences

Speaker, entrepreneur and designer are packaged into one clear hierarchy.

Process

How I built it

I started with the structure: who she is → what she can do → what she has achieved → what she talks about → how to contact. Repeated slides from this block have been removed because they are already shown above.
