Approach

How I work with AI

01

First the task, then the tool

AI is used not for the sake of effect, but where it reduces manual work, speeds up analysis, or helps assemble a working prototype.

02

Visual and logic together

For me, an AI case is not just a picture or a prompt, but a combination of an interface, a script, a data structure and a result for the user.

03

Verifiable result

What is important is not the promises, but the working scenario: what the user enters, what the user receives as output, and how it can be applied.
